About BOSSB CO

BOSSB CO creates premium bouquets for birthdays, anniversaries, celebrations, and everyday surprises. We focus on thoughtful design and clean presentation — so every order feels like a moment, not just a product.

Meet the founder

The story behind the brand

Thekgo Bokgobelo started BOSSB CO to change how people experience gifting — making flowers feel intentional, personal, and unforgettable.

Every arrangement is designed with emotion in mind — premium finishing, thoughtful detail, and a smooth ordering flow that respects your time.

What we offer

  • Hand-tied bouquets with clean wrapping
  • Custom colours and sizes (depending on stock)
  • Add-ons: chocolates, card note, vase (where available)
  • Local delivery in selected Johannesburg areas

How ordering works

  1. Browse bouquets and choose your favourite
  2. Customise size, colour, and add-ons
  3. Add to cart and review your order
  4. Send your order on WhatsApp from the cart

Why it feels premium

  • Design-led arrangements (not random bunches)
  • Gift-ready finishing and neat presentation
  • Quick confirmations so you're never guessing
  • Careful substitutions if a specific stem is unavailable

Delivery & Ordering Policy

Read full policy →

Delivery areas

We deliver to selected areas within Johannesburg. Delivery availability and fees depend on distance and are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Delivery times

Operating hours: Mon–Sat, 08:00–18:00. Same-day slots may be available depending on stock and your area.

Ordering & payment

Orders are placed via WhatsApp (from your cart). We confirm bouquet availability, delivery options, delivery fee, and the final total before payment. Prices may vary slightly based on seasonal flowers and availability.

Returns & substitutions

Because flowers are perishable, returns are not accepted. If a specific flower or colour is unavailable, we may suggest a suitable substitution of equal or greater value.
